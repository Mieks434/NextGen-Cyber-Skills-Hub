Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Our NextGen Cyber Skills Hub values the privacy of our users and we are committed to protect your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). POPIA principles that apply to our website is:

  • Accountability: Responsibility of using user’s data lawfully.
  • Processing: Data is handled legally, fairly and only when it is necessary.
  • Purpose Specification: Data is collected for clear, specific purposes and not kept longer than needed.
  • Further Processing Limtation: User’s data cannot be reused for new purposes unless it is compatible or user’s consent is renewed
  • Information Quality: User’s data must be accurate, complete and up to date.
  • Openness: User’s are informed about data collection, why it is being collected and how the data will be processed.
  • Security Safeguards: Data od users are being protected against loss, damage or unauthorized access.
  • Data Subject Participation: Users have the right to access, correct or delete their data.

NextGen Cyber Skills Hub make use of minimal use of personal data (Full Name, Email Address and the message content that is sent to us) for the use of our contact form, when you want to reach out to us.
